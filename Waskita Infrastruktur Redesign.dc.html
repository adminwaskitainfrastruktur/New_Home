A complete rebuild of waskitainfrastruktur.co.id on the 2026 brand system — new information architecture, a national project map with real data, an equipment rental catalogue, and a responsive front-end ready for JSX/TSX.

What we are fixing

The current site runs on a page builder with nine top-level menus and dropdowns four levels deep. Content is largely flat images, the project list is an unstructured stack of 36 headings, and the layout does not hold together on a phone. The eight items below are the problems this redesign is measured against.

01
Navigation too deep

Nine top menus, four nesting levels, duplicated destinations.

02
Weak project showcase

36 projects with no filtering, no map, no data, no detail pages.

03
Governance not visible

GCG, WBS and KIP buried under two generic menus.

04
News hard to read

Carousel of identical cards, no categories, no dates.

05
Rental business invisible

412 units of equipment for hire, with no catalogue or enquiry route.

06
Poor on mobile

Desktop layouts scaled down; text set in images.

07
Inconsistent identity

Brand colours applied ad hoc; no type or spacing system.

08
Speed & accessibility

Heavy builder output, image-only text, low contrast.

Overview

Delivered ahead of the contracted target

Sections 1 and 2 of the Palembang–Betung toll road form part of the Trans-Sumatra corridor, connecting the provincial capital to the Betung junction and shortening travel time across South Sumatra.

The package covered earthworks, rigid and flexible pavement, drainage, bridges and overpasses, along with toll facilities. Work was completed ahead of the contracted target, with handover confirmed in 2025.

Recovery Berbuah Hasil, WKI Raih Laba dan Kontrak Baru 2025

12 February 2026Corporate SecretariatShare
In this release
Return to profit in 2025 New contract position Outlook for 2026
Media contact

Corporate Secretariat
info.wki@waskitainfrastruktur.co.id

Jakarta — PT Waskita Karya Infrastruktur closed the 2025 financial year with a return to profit and a strengthened new-contract position, marking the end of a two-year recovery programme.

The company attributed the result to tighter project selection, cost discipline across its four business lines, and the completion of several packages ahead of contracted schedule. Section 2 of the Palembang–Betung toll road was handed over earlier than target, while work on BOCIMI Section 3 and the IKN Nusantara packages progressed on plan.

Management noted that the energy portfolio is expected to contribute from 2026, as the 50 MW hydro capacity held through PT Waskita Wado Energi moves towards operation. The steel fabrication and heavy equipment units are also expected to increase their share of external revenue.

“The recovery is not a single year of numbers. It is a change in how we choose and run projects.”

Board of Directors

Governance improvements were recognised externally during the year, with two awards received at TOP GRC Awards 2025. The company continues to operate certified quality, environmental and occupational health and safety management systems.
